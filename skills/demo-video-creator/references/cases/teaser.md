# Case: teaser

12–20s pre-launch or social teaser, or a short cutdown of a finished film. **Music-led**, 3–5 of the strongest real
shots, one line of VO at most, ends on a date or a URL.

- Length: 12–20s (`qa_check.py … 12 20 3`).
- Theme: the product's theme.
- Music: teaser (build → big final hit → silence), or the launch bed's drop.

## Shape (15s)
| # | s | beat | picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–2 | strongest moving shot, full-bleed (**big hit** on frame 6) | real output / the product's money moment |
| 2 | 2–8 | 3 more shots, one per bar (not per beat — no strobes) | each with real motion inside |
| 3 | 8–11 | one line, word by word ("Stop guessing.") | `headline` |
| 4 | 11–13 | music stops; mark settles in silence | `logoReveal` |
| 5 | 13–15 | final hit: date / URL (**big hit**) | `endCard` |

## Rules specific to teasers
- VO: none, or ONE line (≤ 8 words) in the one-take style. Music-led films still put words on beats, 2–4f apart.
- Never reveal what isn't built; a teaser may hide, never fake.
- One shot per BAR (≈2s at 120 BPM), not per beat: 0.1–0.5s footage strobes were rejected ("영상 막 쏟아져 나오는거").
- 2–3 big hits only (shot 1, the silence → end hit). The silence before the last hit is the moment; keep it ≥ 1 beat.
- Cutdown from a master: pick shots from the master's captures, re-render at the teaser's own beat grid.
